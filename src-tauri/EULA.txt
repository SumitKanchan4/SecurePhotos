SecurePhotos: before you install

SecurePhotos is free software under the MIT License, which you'll find at the end of this page. Here is what it means for you, in plain words.

YOUR PHOTOS ARE YOURS
Everything you add to SecurePhotos stays yours. The app keeps your photos, videos and documents on your own drive and never uploads them anywhere. Nobody else gets any rights to them.

KEEP YOUR OWN BACKUPS
SecurePhotos takes care not to lose anything: it copies your photos when you import them, keeps the original when it improves a photo (unless you choose otherwise), and holds anything you delete in Trash for 30 days. Still, any drive can fail. Keep a copy of your original photos and videos on a separate drive.

YOUR PRIVATE VAULT
Only you know your vault password. If you forget it, the Emergency Card you save during setup is the only way back in. Without the password or the card, nobody can open the vault, including the author of SecurePhotos.

NO WARRANTY
